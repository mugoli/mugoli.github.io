I'm Mugoli.

I’m a freelance writer, editorial consultant and creative strategist splitting my time between Toronto, Canada and London, England.

I’m currently accepting select clients across editorial strategy, marketing, pr, journalism and more. Learn more about my services here.

I previously worked as an editor for Apple Canada’s bilingual news service, Apple News. I led major, cross-services editorial campaigns and managed their three weekly newsletters. I also worked in breaking news – curating daily news lineups, liaising with international publishers, and keeping millions of users informed. The role involved sharp editorial judgment, cross-functional collaboration skills, project management, quick thinking, and a deep understanding of user analytics. This was a bilingual role (je suis franco-ontarienne et parfaitement bilingue). I understand how to serve audiences in different linguistic and cultural markets.

At Apple, I also hosted and moderated fireside chats with figures such as Sophie Grégoire Trudeau, Maestro Fresh Wes and more.

Before joining Apple, I worked as a reporter for some of Canada’s largest legacy media organizations: The Globe and Mail and Radio-Canada/CBC. Print. TV. Radio. Web. Sharp storytelling, extremely tight deadlines. You get the gist.

My longform writing has appeared in 3 Magazine, Broadview Magazine and more. I’ve won a Canadian Church Press Award and was nominated for a National Magazine Award. I'm a National Magazine Awards juror.

Here’s the tally of my experience: I have a keen eye for how to find and tell a good story. I understand what keeps audiences engaged and how to connect with them across platforms. I know media and tech from the inside out. And, above all else, I genuinely like people. I’m outside. This will matter to those who understand.

I’m a lifelong lover of music, arts, culture, good adventures, life. I’m a bassist. I’m also the founder of an ice-skating club. I’m also the host of informal listening parties. Some people know me as the founder of a music blog, or as the person who flipped a barber shop and turned it into an art gallery. As the person who hopped off a tall ship and swam in the St. Lawrence River. As, as, as. But those are stories for another time.
